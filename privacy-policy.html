Privacy Policy

This app accesses TikTok account data solely to authenticate the account owner and publish video clips on their behalf via TikTok's Content Posting API.

Data accessed: TikTok account authorization tokens (used only to authenticate API requests).

Data NOT collected: no personal information about TikTok viewers, followers, or other users is collected or stored.

Data retention: authorization tokens are stored securely and only for as long as needed to operate the app. You may revoke access at any time via your TikTok account settings.

This app does not sell or share any data with third parties.
