Terms of Service

This app is a personal project that identifies highlight moments from a Twitch livestream and posts short video clips to TikTok on behalf of its owner's own TikTok account, with the streamer's permission.

This app does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to TikTok users beyond what is required to authenticate and post to the developer's own connected account. No content is posted without manual human review and approval prior to upload.

By using this app, the account owner authorizes it to publish video content to their TikTok account via TikTok's Content Posting API.

For questions, contact: twitchtiktokclipsbot@gmail.com
