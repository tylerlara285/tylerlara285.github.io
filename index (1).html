Best moments from StableRonaldo's streams

An automated highlight system that finds the funniest and most talked-about moments from live streams and shares them as short vertical clips.

What this is

StableRonaldo Clips (@clipsofstableronaldo on TikTok) is an automated tool that identifies highlight moments from StableRonaldo's Twitch streams and posts short clips to TikTok, with the streamer's permission. Chat activity from each stream is analyzed to find moments that got a genuine reaction — a funny mistake, a surprising reveal, a big win — and those moments get turned into short vertical clips.

All clips are reviewed and approved by a person before posting. Nothing goes out automatically without a human checking it first.

Why this site exists

This page exists to explain what the account does and to host the legal pages required for the tools this project uses to post content on our own behalf. It isn't a login portal or a product — just documentation for this specific project.
